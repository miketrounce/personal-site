The South African Government Bond Market

Summary

  • Government behaviour reveals that supply is de-facto fixed at 4.5% of GDP / year
  • Non-fundamental demand is variable, as revealed by foreign investor behaviour and price swings
  • Explosive debt dynamics could impact fundamental demand in nonlinear ways

Supply & Demand

  • My supply schedule is steep and fixed
  • Steep: changes in borrowing costs (bond yields) have little impact
  • Fixed: neither appetite for austerity nor go-for-growth
  • Fits with significant variation observed in bond yields, caused by demand variation
The South African Government Bond Market

Supply (Currency)

  • South Africa borrows in both local currency (ZAR) and foreign currency
  • Local currency debt dominates: consistently ~90% of the total
  • Foreign currency debt is small but growing (ZAR 100bn → 600bn)
  • The rest of this presentation focuses on the local currency market

Demand (Flow)
