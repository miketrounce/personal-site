Flows identify which side moved — the empirical handle on an identification problem.

  • Looking at yield alone, you can't tell whether a supply shock or a demand shock moved the market — both raise yields.
  • Pairing the yield response with flow data (who bought, who sold, by how much) identifies the shock.
  • This is the elastic-hypothesis framework applied empirically — combining yields and quantities to back out the shock.
  • Flows + positions = the empirical handle on a fundamentally identification-hard problem. The rest of this site is the data infrastructure that makes that handle real.
