Bond market: supply and demand in yield space

  • The bond market plots in yield space — y-axis is yield, not price. Demand slopes UP because bonds at higher yields are cheaper, so investors want to buy more of them.
  • Supply is near-vertical — the government issues what the deficit requires, with little sensitivity to where yields clear.
  • Equilibrium yield is set where (near-vertical) supply meets (upward-sloping) demand. Because supply barely shifts with yield, demand-side flows do most of the moving when yields change.
