GBI-EM country weights are the passive-neutral baseline.

  • One index, ~16 countries. GBI-EM Global Diversified covers the major EM local-currency sovereign markets.
  • 10% per-country cap. Without it, China and a handful of others would dominate. The cap forces a flatter, more diversified neutral.
  • Neutral = index weight. A fund at SA's index weight is taking no view on SA. Anything else is an active position, by construction.
  • Why GBI-EM. 90%+ of dedicated EM local funds benchmark to it. It's the universe's shared coordinate system.
