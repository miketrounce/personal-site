Shifts in supply and demand: where shocks move the equilibrium.

  • A supply shock shifts the supply curve sideways — e.g. a new entrant, productivity gain, regulatory change.
  • A demand shock shifts the demand curve sideways — e.g. a tax rebate, change in tastes, income shift.
  • Either shift produces a new equilibrium at a different (P*, Q*).
  • How far each variable moves depends on the slopes — the elasticities — of both curves.
