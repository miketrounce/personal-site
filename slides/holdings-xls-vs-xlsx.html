Pre-2023 files are .xls. The loader converts before parsing.

  • RSA Investor Relations files predating ~2023 ship as .xls — the legacy BIFF binary format.
  • xlrd<2.0 chokes on these with an SST-table assertion; pandas’ default Excel reader fails.
  • The loader normalises every input through soffice --headless --convert-to xlsx (libreoffice-calc on the droplet) before parsing.
  • Every artefact archived as a ReleaseFile is .xlsx — the original .xls is only a transient input, never stored.
