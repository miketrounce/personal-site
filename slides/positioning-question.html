Where is foreign money positioned in EM local-currency bonds?

  • The question is not how much foreign money owns EM bonds — it is where that money is leaning, country by country.
  • Allocation alone is hard to interpret. A 10% allocation to Mexico is huge for a small country, normal if Mexico is 10% of the index.
  • Subtracting the benchmark turns allocation into a relative signal: deviation from passive. That's what an active position is.
