Government fiscal accounts sit at nested institutional perimeters

  • Fiscal accounts are reported at nested institutional perimeters — concentric rings of "what counts as the government." Each perimeter prints its own deficit, debt stock, and fiscal balance because each draws the boundary in a different place.
  • The narrowest ring is the central treasury; outer rings progressively absorb sub-national governments, social-security funds, then state-owned enterprises.
  • Strict nesting always holds — every unit inside an inner ring is also inside every outer ring.
