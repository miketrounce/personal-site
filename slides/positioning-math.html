AI-generated Mike Trounce · Fund allocation as a positioning signal

Position = Allocation − Benchmark, expressed in percentage points.

  • Subtraction, not ratio. Position is in percentage points, not a multiple. +7pp means seven points above the index, not 7×.
  • Sign carries direction. Positive = overweight (real money is leaning long the country). Negative = underweight.
  • Magnitude carries conviction. +0.5pp is a fund roster that's barely off the index. +7pp is a meaningful collective lean.
  • Universe-summed, AUM-weighted. Each step — allocation, benchmark, position — is computed across the whole universe at month-end. Big funds dominate the average.
