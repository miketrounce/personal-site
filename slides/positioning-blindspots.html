AI-generated Mike Trounce · Fund allocation as a positioning signal

The signal is precise, narrow, and lagged. Know its blind spots.

  • Buyer-universe gaps. Central bank reserves, crossovers, sovereign wealth, hedge funds — none in the universe. The signal speaks for one slice, not for “foreigners” as a whole.
  • Monthly grain, ~3-week lag. Position you read today reflects month-end three weeks ago. A fund pivot mid-month is invisible until the following month-end.
  • Universe drift. Funds launch, close, change mandate. The 2016 universe is not exactly the 2026 universe; long-run comparisons need a grain of salt.
  • Pair with flows. For total foreign positioning, sit this signal alongside higher-frequency flow series (SABO, EPFR) — each catches what the other misses.
