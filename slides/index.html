01

Schedule of Domestic Government Bonds — monthly PDFs since May 2003.

Overview — NT publication, monthly cadence, 263 releases. Each release lists every domestic government bond outstanding at month-end with nominal amount, coupon, redemption.

/slides/schedule-overview
02

Each PDF is a bond table. One row per bond.

PDF anatomy — 8 columns (code, coupon, issue date, price, amount issued, interest dates, redemption, nominal); ~50 bond rows per release; stable shape across all 263 releases.

/slides/schedule-pdf-anatomy
03

pypdf line-by-line. Regex matches each bond row.

Parser — pypdf reads each page as text, walk lines top-to-bottom, regex identifies bond rows by their leading code, tokenize coupon → dates → amounts.

/slides/schedule-pypdf-parsing
04

Asterisks on the bond code carry the instrument type.

Bond classification — ** = inflation-linked, *** = floating-rate, * = three-maturity, **** = repo at all-in price. Loader sets BondSchedule.bond_type from the marks.

/slides/schedule-asterisks-and-instrument-types
05

R2044, R2048, I2050 print as three rows each — collapsed to one bond.

Bond identity normalisation — three SA bonds redeem in equal thirds across three dates; the PDF prints them as three separate rows, the loader collapses to canonical bond code.

/slides/schedule-three-maturity-collapse
06

From one rollup release to 263 monthly releases.

Release-grain split — the original rollup buried the May 2024 RN2027 parser bug; the per-month split gives every PDF its own Release with reference_period set to the month-end.

/slides/schedule-per-month-split
07

Per-bond outstanding = Σ holdings. The same invariant from the other side.

Validation invariant — Schedule’s outstanding stock per bond per month-end equals the Holdings xlsx sum across nine holders, to the rand. Surfaced the May 2024 RN2027 bug.

/slides/schedule-recon-vs-holdings
