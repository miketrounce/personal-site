Bond price and bond yield are inverses.

  • A bond is a stream of fixed cashflows: coupons over time + face value at maturity.
  • Yield is the discount rate that makes those cashflows present-value to today's bond price.
  • Pay more for the same cashflows, you earn less. Higher price ⇔ lower yield.
  • Bond markets quote in yield, not price — yield is unit-comparable across bonds with different coupons and maturities.
